You'd think we'd grow out of it. We don't.

The beliefs we soaked up on playgrounds and screens don't disappear when we start paying rent. They go quiet — and then they show up in the big stuff. Who we picture as "the leader." Who we assume will handle the childcare. Who we expect to have "settled down" by now.

Most of it runs on autopilot. Nobody decides to call an assertive woman "bossy" and an assertive man "driven" — it just happens, fast, below the surface.

The good news is the same as it was in primary school: anything learned can be unlearned. You notice the pattern. You question it. You choose differently next time.

Unlearn. Relearn. Grow.
