## Critical path

1. **Now.** Register on NGO Darpan. Record the entity details. Fill the [budget inputs](budget.md#inputs-needed). Complete the case for support's track record. Fix the website items in [readiness](readiness.md#5-what-funders-see-on-our-website).
2. **By 1 October.** Decide whether to run a small Daan Utsav appeal (2 to 8 October) to our own network. Without 80G there is no tax benefit, and the appeal should say so.
3. **When 12A and 80G arrive.** Open /donate for Indian payments only, file CSR-1, put registration details on the site, and start applying to Indian foundations.
4. **Every year after 31 March.** Close the books and get them audited. Direct CSR grants and FCRA are both measured in audited years, so each audit is a fundraising asset.

## Open inputs from the founders

- Entity type (Trust, Society or Section 8 company), registration number and date, and the date the 12A and 80G applications went in.
- Delivery to date: partners, sessions, unique children, locations, and any pre/post results.
- The rates listed in [budget.md](budget.md#inputs-needed).
- Whether we describe ourselves as women-led. The site lists three women co-founders, and several gender funds ask.

## Rules for all fundraising material

- **No photographs of children, ever**, including for a CSR report. Offer materials, the team at work, approved illustrations and real numbers instead.
- **Only claims we can defend.** Single-group pre/post supports a claim about stated knowledge, attitudes and confidence. Not about behaviour.
- **Re-verify legal facts before anything goes to a funder.** Tax, CSR and FCRA rules changed in 2020, 2021 and 2026. Each file carries the date its facts were checked.
- **No individual donor's contact details or PAN in this repo.** Git history cannot be deleted. Those live in the donor records sheet.
- House voice: no em dashes, concise, limitations stated plainly.
