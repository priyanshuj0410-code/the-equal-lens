# Programme budget and cost per child

Related: [case for support](case-for-support.md#the-ask) · [funder map](funder-map.md)

Every ask should be in units a donor can picture: one child, one class, one partner for a year. This file turns delivery facts into those units. None of the rates here is an estimate. Each one comes from the founders, with its source noted.

## Unit of delivery

One **cohort**: a single group at one partner, taken through one full programme, one session a week.

| Programme | Sessions | Session length | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery (5 to 12) | 8 | 1 hr 40 min | 8 |
| Teen Compass (13 to 15) | 7 | 1 hr 40 min. Session 7 runs 1 hr 50 | 7 |
| Relay (16 to 18) | 6 | 1 hr 40 min. Session 6 runs 1 hr 50 | 6 |

The partner provides the room and its safeguarding lead at no cost to us.

## Cost lines

| Line | Charged per | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| Lead facilitator | session | Fee or salary share, including preparation time |
| Second facilitator | session | If group size or safeguarding needs two of us in the room |
| Travel | session | Round trip to the partner |
| Materials | child | Mission cards, print-and-cut kits, stationery, certificate (Discovery) |
| Measurement | child, plus analysis per cohort | Pre/post forms, printing, analysis |
| Partner coordination | cohort | Onboarding, safeguarding briefing, scheduling, closing report |
| Facilitator readiness | facilitator per year | Training, police verification and background checks |
| Running costs | year | Audit and compliance, payment gateway fees, website, programme management, insurance if any |

## Formulas

- **Direct cost per cohort** = sessions × (lead + second facilitator + travel) + children × (materials + measurement) + coordination
- **Running-cost share per cohort** = (running costs + facilitator readiness) ÷ cohorts delivered in the year
- **Full cost per cohort** = direct cost + running-cost share
- **Cost per child** = full cost per cohort ÷ children per cohort

Always quote the full cost. A cost per child that leaves out running costs looks good this year and leaves us short next year.

## Gift units

Filled in once the inputs are in, then copied to the [case for support](case-for-support.md#the-ask) and the donate page.

| Unit | Formula |
|---|---|
| One child through Discovery | Cost per child, Discovery |
| One session for a class | Full cost per cohort ÷ sessions |
| One class through Discovery | Full cost per cohort, Discovery |
| One partner for a year | Sum of that partner's cohorts |

## Not in this budget

- **Adult workshops** (workplace, campus, community). Priced separately, as paid work.
- **Unpaid founder time.** If the founders are unpaid now, say so in proposals and include paid time from the first grant. An honest full-cost budget is more credible than an artificially cheap one.
