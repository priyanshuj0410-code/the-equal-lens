# Funder pipeline

Related: [funder map](funder-map.md) · [readiness](readiness.md)

Record organisations here: foundations, grantmakers, companies, platforms, government offices. Keep individual donors in the donor records sheet, not in this repo, because git history cannot be deleted.

## Stages

| Stage | Means | Moves on when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Identified | A name worth checking | Fit and eligibility are checked |
| 2. Qualified | Fits our work, and we meet its requirements or know the date we will | First contact is made |
| 3. In conversation | We are talking to a person | They invite a proposal, or say no |
| 4. Proposal sent | Application or proposal submitted | A decision arrives |
| 5. Won | Agreement signed | Reporting dates are set |
| 6. Stewardship | Reporting, thanking, showing the work | Renewal ask |
| Parked | Not now. Always carries a date to revisit | That date |
| Declined | They said no. Note the reason | |

## Fields

- **Eligibility now:** Open · After 80G · After three audited years · After FCRA
- **Type:** one of the seven in the [funder map](funder-map.md)
- **Route in:** warm introduction, open call, platform, or cold approach
- **Ask:** amount in ₹, and the [gift unit](budget.md#gift-units) it buys
